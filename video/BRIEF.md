## Intent

A promotional video for the T3 Loupe GitHub README. Chosen concept (#5, "The loupe"): a literal
jeweller's loupe glides across a dense, dimmed desktop; wherever it stops, hidden detail swims into
focus under the glass — the exact command awaiting approval, 73% context, Codex at 100% for 47 more
minutes — playing directly on the product's name. Closes on the full two-pane panel and the install
line. Must read without sound (README videos play muted).
